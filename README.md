##  What is Minecraft?

Minecraft is a popular sandbox video game developed by Mojang Studios. It was officially released in 2011 and allows players to explore, build, craft, and survive in a world made entirely of blocks.

##  Did You Know?

- Creepers were created because of a coding mistake when designing pigs.

- Minecraft worlds can be incredibly large.

- Endermen can pick up certain blocks.

- The Minecraft soundtrack was composed by Daniel Rosenfeld, also known as C418.
